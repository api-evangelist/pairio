# Pairio

> Pairio is an AI-powered maintenance assistant for manufacturing and industrial teams. It helps technicians troubleshoot machine breakdowns, search technical documentation, document maintenance work, identify spare parts, analyze recurring failures, and create work instructions.

## Product

- [Pairio homepage](https://pairio.com/): AI troubleshooting and maintenance assistant for industrial teams. Also available in German at https://pairio.com/de/.
- [Blog](https://pairio.com/blog/): Practical writing on maintenance knowledge, recurring plant problems, downtime, and industrial maintenance workflows.
- [Full content for LLMs](https://pairio.com/llms-full.txt): Complete text of this site's marketing and blog content in one file.

## Key Use Cases

- Troubleshoot machine breakdowns faster
- Reduce mean time to repair (MTTR)
- Search technical manuals, wiring diagrams, SOPs, and maintenance history
- Capture technician knowledge from voice, photo, and video
- Generate structured maintenance reports
- Identify spare parts used during repairs
- Analyze recurring maintenance issues and optimization potential
- Create work instructions and onboarding material
- Preserve expert knowledge across shifts, sites, and teams

## Buyer Fit

Pairio is built for manufacturers and industrial companies with internal maintenance teams, from mid-sized businesses to large enterprises. It is especially relevant for teams using ERP, CMMS, EAM, document management systems, SharePoint, SAP, IFS, IBM Maximo, Oracle EAM, Asset APIs, technical documentation, machine logbooks, or maintenance history.

## Integrations

Pairio integrates with common ERP systems and document management systems. Publicly shown integration examples include SAP, IFS, IBM Maximo, Oracle EAM Cloud, Hexagon, Asset API, SharePoint, and Outlook.
